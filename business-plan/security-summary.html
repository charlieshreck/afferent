Security & Data Handling

How We Protect Your Data

For decision makers — April 2026

This document explains how Afferentic handles your data, where it lives, and what controls are in place to keep it safe. For the full technical detail, see the Technical Security Annex.

Data Residency

Where Your Data Lives

  • UK/EU hosted — our infrastructure runs in Hetzner data centres in Germany, fully GDPR-compliant. Your data never leaves the UK/EU.
  • Isolated environment — your data lives in a separate namespace with its own database and its own encryption keys. This is not a shared database with access controls bolted on — it is architecturally separate.
  • No other client can access your data — this isolation is enforced at the infrastructure level by network policies that block cross-tenant traffic entirely. It is not dependent on people following rules; the technology prevents it.

Encryption

What We Encrypt

In Transit

TLS 1.3 everywhere — from your browser to our platform, and from our platform to your systems. This is the same encryption used by your bank.

At Rest

AES-256 disk encryption on all storage volumes. This means that even if a physical disk were removed from the data centre, the data on it would be unreadable.

Backups

Encrypted separately and stored with a different provider from the main platform. If the primary infrastructure were compromised, backups would remain safe.

Access Control

What We Access

  • Only what you explicitly authorise — every connection uses the vendor’s own consent mechanism (Microsoft, Xero, etc.). You see exactly what’s requested before granting access.
  • You control permissions — add or revoke access at any time from your own admin portals. No call to us required.
  • We never see or store your passwords — all authentication uses OAuth2, which means you authenticate directly with your provider. Afferentic receives a token; never your credentials.

Minimum scope principle: we request only the permissions needed for the specific outcomes you’ve agreed to. If an outcome doesn’t need email access, we don’t request it.

Audit Trail

What We Log

  • Every action logged — every action our system takes on your behalf is recorded: what was done, when, why, and what the result was.
  • Independent log storage — logs are stored on a separate, independent monitoring system. They cannot be modified or deleted from the main platform.
  • 6-year retention — logs are retained for six years to meet compliance requirements.
  • Available on request — you can request access to your audit logs at any time.

When Things Go Wrong

Incident Response

  • Client notification within 1 hour of discovering a security incident affecting your data.
  • ICO notification within 72 hours where required by GDPR.
  • Professional indemnity and cyber liability insurance in place to cover the costs of any incident.

Proactive, not reactive. Our monitoring system checks platform health every 15 minutes. Most issues are detected and resolved before they affect you.

Credentials

Certifications

  • Cyber Essentials Plus certified — the UK government-backed standard covering five key security controls: firewalls, secure configuration, access control, malware protection, and patch management.
  • CREST-accredited penetration test — completed by an independent, accredited security firm before first client engagement.
  • SOC 2 Type II observation — begins in Year 2 of trading, providing independent verification of our security controls over time.

Exit

When You Leave

  • Full data export — provided within 30 days of your engagement ending, in standard open formats.
  • Complete data purge — all your data is deleted with written confirmation that nothing is retained.
  • Credentials rotated — all tokens and access associated with your tenant are revoked and rotated.
  • No proprietary lock-in — we use standard APIs throughout. Your data and processes are yours.
