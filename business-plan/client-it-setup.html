Client IT Setup Guide

Getting Started with Afferentic

For your IT administrator — April 2026

Why You’re Receiving This

Your organisation has engaged Afferentic to help improve operational outcomes. Afferentic connects to your existing business systems — Microsoft 365, accounting software, and similar tools — to identify inefficiencies and deliver measurable improvements.

This guide walks your IT administrator through the steps required to grant Afferentic the access it needs. Each connection uses standard vendor-provided authentication (OAuth2), and you retain full control over what’s shared at every stage.

What You’ll Need

  • Microsoft 365 admin access — Global Admin or Application Admin role
  • About 10 minutes — the initial portal consent takes under 2 minutes; the rest is optional and happens later

Step 1

Portal Access (Entra ID Consent)

This enables your team to log into the Afferentic portal using their existing Microsoft 365 accounts. No new usernames or passwords required.

1

Open the consent URL

Your M365 Global Admin or Application Admin opens the consent link provided by Afferentic. This is a standard Microsoft Entra ID admin consent flow — the same process used by any business application that connects to Microsoft 365.

2

Review the permissions

The consent screen will show the permissions Afferentic is requesting. For portal access, these are:

  • openid — confirms identity (lets us verify the user is who they say they are)
  • profile — reads the user’s display name
  • email — reads the user’s email address

What we see: your name and email address. That’s it.

What we do NOT see: your files, emails, calendar, contacts, Teams messages, or anything else in your Microsoft 365 environment.

3

Click “Accept”

Once the admin clicks Accept, the consent is granted for the entire tenant. Your users can now log into app.afferentic.com using their existing Microsoft 365 credentials.

4

Verify access

Any user in your tenant can now sign in to the Afferentic portal. No additional accounts, licences, or configuration needed. Users authenticate through your own Entra ID — your existing MFA policies apply automatically.

Step 2 (During Engagement)

M365 Data Access

This is separate from portal access and is only requested during the Map or Make engagement — not at signup. Your admin will receive a second consent prompt with these additional permissions.

Permissions Explained

Mail

Mail.Read

Allows Afferentic to read email metadata and content for inbox analysis — identifying communication patterns, response times, and bottlenecks. We analyse patterns in aggregate; we don’t read individual emails for content unless specifically agreed in writing.

Files

Files.Read

Allows Afferentic to read documents in OneDrive and SharePoint — understanding document workflows, naming conventions, version control practices, and where information lives. We look at structure and usage patterns, not the content of private documents.

Users

Users.Read.All

Allows Afferentic to read the organisation directory — understanding team structure, reporting lines, and licence allocation. This helps identify collaboration patterns and unused software licences.

You can review and revoke any permission at any time from your Microsoft 365 admin portal under Enterprise Applications → Afferentic → Permissions.

Step 3 (If Applicable)

Xero Connection

During the Map engagement, if financial analysis is part of the agreed scope, Afferentic will request a connection to your Xero account.

How It Works

  • OAuth2 authentication — you sign in directly with Xero. Afferentic never sees your Xero password.
  • Read access only — Afferentic can read your chart of accounts, invoices, contacts, and bank transactions.
  • No payment capability — Afferentic cannot create, modify, or authorise any payments or transactions.
  • Disconnect at any time — revoke access instantly from Xero’s Settings → Connected Apps page.

We access your accounting software, not your bank. The Xero connection lets us analyse invoicing patterns, payment cycles, and financial workflows. We never have access to your bank credentials, and we cannot move money.

Control

Revoking Access

You retain full control over every connection. Here’s how to remove access at any time.

Portal Access

Open your Microsoft 365 admin portal → Enterprise Applications → find Afferentic → click Delete. This immediately revokes all users’ ability to sign into the Afferentic portal.

M365 Data Permissions

Same location: Enterprise Applications → Afferentic → Permissions. You can revoke specific permissions individually or remove the entire application.

Xero

Open Xero → Settings → Connected Apps → find Afferentic → click Disconnect.

On Exit

When your engagement with Afferentic ends, we purge all client data within 30 days. You receive written confirmation that all data has been deleted, all credentials have been rotated, and no copies are retained.
